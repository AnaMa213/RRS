## R1 — Forbidden adjacency can authorize a maneuver

**Severity:** high. **Route:** patch. **Sources:** blind-hunter + edge-case-hunter. **Requirement:** M1 and authored lateral legality. **Location:** `Assets/RoadRage/Features/Vehicles/Traffic/Tactical/ManeuverEvaluation.cs:437`.

`TryAdjacent` accepts a lateral relation without reading `LaneAdjacency.Permission`. `RoadModelCompiler.cs:131` preserves all authored adjacencies, including `Forbidden` (`RoadModelRecords.cs:242`). A contiguous, otherwise feasible forbidden connection is evaluated exactly like an allowed one and can become `Selected`. Neither the proof nor the subsequent safety check restores the permission gate; only opposing-corridor candidates request an exception.

**Correction:** exclude forbidden adjacencies before candidate evaluation. Add a Story542 case whose geometry is feasible but whose adjacency is forbidden; require no adjacent departure or implicit legalization.

## R2 — Adjacency coverage ends at the cause instead of covering the maneuver

**Severity:** high. **Route:** patch. **Sources:** blind-hunter + edge-case-hunter + verification-gap. **Requirement:** M1, an adjacency covering the maneuver extent. **Location:** `ManeuverEvaluation.cs:437-438`.

The domain check covers only `SelfSMeters..Cause.FarSMeters`. Geometry subsequently places the return after the cause, longitudinal clearance and half the vehicle length, then adds a return transition and settling tail. The proof receives corridor envelopes, not the adjacency, and never verifies its source/target domains. Thus an adjacency ending at `Cause.FarSMeters` passes this gate while the return crossing is outside its authored extent. Target-side `ToStartSMeters`/`ToEndSMeters` are also ignored.

**Correction:** validate the authored source/target extent against the actual maneuver, including the return, after constructing it; fail closed when authorization does not cover it. Preserve direction/permission semantics for the return. Test partial coverage and asymmetric source/target intervals. The current adjacent fixture uses full-corridor intervals (`Story542ManeuverTests.cs:783-787`).

## R3 — Unavailable maneuver perception still produces propulsion

**Severity:** high. **Route:** patch. **Source:** blind-hunter. **Requirement:** fail-closed perception and the single safe driving stack. **Locations:** `TrafficV2VehicleDriver.cs:970-974`; `ManeuverEvaluation.cs:506-514`; `TacticalDecision.cs:558`.

`NearField` deliberately publishes `ChannelUnavailable`, `ChannelSaturated` or `HazardCollectorSaturated`. Both departure validation and an active maneuver nevertheless construct a command from its retained obstacle list without checking `UnavailableReason`. Unlike nominal `LongitudinalArbitration.cs:488-490`, this path does not insert a perception-unavailable constraint. `SafetyFilter.cs:193-194` inspects retained hazards but does not gate on availability, and `Intent/VehicleDriveIntentComposer.cs:158-172` translates any finite valid supplied command even if a refusal is supplied alongside it. A truncated observation can therefore permit departure or continued acceleration.

**Correction:** handle unavailable/saturated maneuver perception before producing a usable maneuver command, with an explicit fail-closed outcome. Apply the same contract at candidate admission and during following. Keep SafetyFilter unchanged. Test unavailable and saturated inputs, including collector saturation, and verify the command/intent cannot accelerate from incomplete observations.

## R4 — The built path does not enforce D2's lateral-acceleration ceiling

**Severity:** medium. **Route:** patch. **Sources:** blind-hunter + acceptance-auditor. **Requirement:** M2 and D2/D3, the approved 2.0 m/s² ceiling. **Locations:** `ManeuverEvaluation.cs:298-305`; `Planning/ManeuverPath.cs:398-401`.

The transition length is derived from the lateral offset's curvature bound. On a curved road, `ManeuverPath.Build` combines road curvature and the offset transition. The completed-path proof checks actual samples against admission curvature and nominal steering speed, but never against `v_m² * abs(kappa_path) <= 2.0`. These are different bounds: a path can satisfy steering and admission while exceeding the approved lateral-acceleration limit. The curvature assertion in `Story542ManeuverTests.cs:337` runs on the straight synthetic corridors at `:770-808`.

**Correction:** include actual combined curvature in the speed/transition feasibility decision and proof. Test a curved corridor where admission/steering pass but the lateral limit fails at the requested speed. Keep the approved D2 value unchanged.

## R5 — A changed cause bypasses D1 while an exception is pending

**Severity:** medium. **Route:** patch. **Source:** edge-case-hunter. **Requirement:** D1, two seconds of the same binding cause. **Location:** `TrafficV2VehicleDriver.cs:1108-1123`.

If A triggered a request and `awaitingException` is true, the next `ManeuverTrigger.Observe` may switch its cause to B and return false because B has just appeared. The following `if (!ready && !awaitingException)` does not return. `TryCause` then reads the new B and a full evaluation can start a maneuver or issue another request immediately, without B satisfying the two-second delay. Geometry/safety reevaluation does not repair this trigger violation.

**Correction:** associate the pending wait with its original cause and invalidate that wait when the binding cause changes. Test A waiting for an exception, B becoming binding at N+1, no evaluation/departure for B until its own D1 interval completes.

## R6 — Incoming corridor connections are absent from conflict prediction

**Severity:** high. **Route:** patch. **Sources:** blind-hunter + verification-gap. **Requirement:** M3, actors on the candidate corridor and its upstream element. **Location:** `ManeuverEvaluation.cs:394-405`.

`Arrival` enumerates upstream `JunctionMovement` occupants only. It never examines `CompiledRoadModel.Connections` (`CompiledRoadModel.cs:462`). An actor on a corridor feeding the candidate through a valid `LaneConnection` is absent from the predicted arrival margin even when its constant-speed arrival precedes maneuver completion. A target corridor being empty does not establish a clear upstream approach.

**Correction:** include immediate incoming corridor connections in the same deterministic arrival calculation, accounting for remaining distance on the predecessor. Test both incoming movement and incoming corridor occupancy with the candidate corridor itself empty. Existing synthetic maneuver models have no movements or connections (`Story542ManeuverTests.cs:790-797`); deleting the current movement loop would also leave those tests unaffected.

## R7 — CorridorOffset ignores faster followers and its upstream actors

**Severity:** high. **Route:** patch. **Sources:** blind-hunter + acceptance-auditor. **Requirement:** M3, candidate-corridor/upstream actor coverage. **Location:** `ManeuverEvaluation.cs:374`; filtering at `:387`.

For the own corridor, `Slack` passes `behindLimit=selfFront` and `upstream=false`. Consequently every follower whose front is behind the ego front is discarded regardless of speed, and incoming movements are skipped. For `CorridorOffset`, that own corridor is the candidate corridor required by M3. A faster follower or upstream actor that reaches the swept region before the maneuver completes can therefore leave slack infinite, rather than refusing departure. The same omission affects the own-corridor return region of other candidates.

**Correction:** calculate candidate/return-region conflicts without dropping approaching actors solely because they are behind the current front. Enable the required upstream scope for CorridorOffset. Test a faster follower and an incoming movement actor, plus a nonconflicting follower to preserve legitimate progression.

## R8 — Gap and risk rejection have no independent regression assertions

**Severity:** medium. **Route:** patch. **Source:** verification-gap. **Requirement:** M3's separate accepted-gap and accepted-risk gates. **Locations:** `Tests/EditMode/Story542ManeuverTests.cs:165-182`; production gates at `ManeuverEvaluation.cs:195-196`.

The named gap/risk fixture accepts either `TrafficConflict` or `PolicyRefused` for its rejecting case, and separately exercises a generous accepted margin. No test asserts `ManeuverPolicyRefusal.Gap` or `.Risk`. Removing the risk rejection would not be caught by a case rejected earlier for negative slack or insufficient gap. This is a verification gap, not a claim that the existing two comparisons are wrong.

**Correction:** add a nonnegative margin below the accepted gap and specifically require `Gap`; separately satisfy the gap but exceed accepted risk and require `Risk`. In both cases assert no selected departure or exception request. Test a nearby accepted boundary to distinguish the two gates.

## R9 — Moving hazard facts lack full constant-velocity prediction

**Severity:** medium. **Route:** patch, following owner clarification. **Sources:** blind-hunter + acceptance-auditor. **Requirement:** M3/M6, explicitly including moving Vehicle/Pedestrian/WalkingPlayer facts and full velocity. **Locations:** `ManeuverEvaluation.cs:411-426` and `:571-572`; `Planning/ManeuverPath.cs:449-451`.

`HazardArrival` checks current hazard bounds only, without `hazard.Velocity`. A moving player car or pedestrian outside the swept band, but entering it during the maneuver, contributes no predicted arrival. For the bypassed cause, `TryCause` retains longitudinal speed only and the proof translates its footprint only longitudinally; lateral motion is lost. NearField/SafetyFilter continue to observe current obstacles, but do not establish the requested future arrival margin.

**Owner decision — 2026-10-09, option 1:** M3 includes moving Vehicle/Pedestrian/WalkingPlayer hazards. Their prediction uses constant world velocity, including lateral motion. The bypassed cause remains excluded from conflict arrival, but its avoidance proof must also preserve lateral motion. The scope clarification is recorded in the approved spec. General wrong-way recognition remains deferred.

**Correction:** compute earliest entry of moving hazard footprints into the swept region using their full constant world velocity, and preserve the bypassed cause's full motion in the avoidance proof. Add crossing-hazard fixtures for Vehicle/Pedestrian/WalkingPlayer, a nonconflicting trajectory, and a bypassed cause with lateral velocity. This decision resolves scope; applying the nine implementation/verification patches remains a separate workflow choice.
