### R2 — high — A changed traversal can reuse an uncovered pending grant

Locations: `Assets/RoadRage/Features/Vehicles/Traffic/Junction/JunctionCoordinator.cs:207` and `:435`. Sources: blind-hunter + edge-case-hunter.

Pending retention checks only `FirstMovementId`, and `CoveredByOwnGrant` returns true immediately for that same identifier. The driver's grant coverage check instead requires every requested movement (`JunctionRequestBuilder.cs:217`). If a replan retains roundabout entry M1 but replaces/adds a continuation or changes the exit, the coordinator retains the old movement list/exit and suppresses the replacement request. The builder can correctly read no full grant, leaving the vehicle constrained at entry indefinitely; a shortened traversal can retain reservations against the old exit.

This is a static logical defect, not a reproduced PlayMode failure. Replanning is an existing supported driver path (`TrafficV2VehicleDriver.cs:694`, `PlanningSpine.cs:102`, `RoutePlanner.TryReuse`), including failure to reuse current progress. Match the complete unengaged traversal/exit before retaining it, and before treating a request as already covered. Add a regression that changes the continuation/exit while preserving the first movement. Preserve the invariant that committed grants are never revoked; this finding does not authorize route-policy changes for committed traversals.

### R3 — medium — O8 engagement is lost on an internal roundabout corridor

Location: `Assets/RoadRage/Features/Vehicles/Traffic/Junction/JunctionRequestBuilder.cs:94`. Source: acceptance-auditor.

The builder starts a chain at the next movement ahead (`:83`). After a committed traversal advances to an internal ring corridor, that movement can be a continuation rather than the original entry. Even though the effective grant covers the complete traversal and is `Held(Committed)`, the builder tests engagement again from distance to this continuation. At low speed and `d >= D_stop`, it makes the already granted suffix the request, rather than skipping the engaged traversal and considering the next junction as required by O8.

Preserve the effective committed traversal's engagement/identity when selecting the next request. The existing O8 test (`Story534JunctionEntryTests.cs:680`) covers commitment while approaching the original entry, not this progression case. Add a real-frame regression on an internal ring corridor with a continuation ahead outside its stopping distance. A downstream unsafe stop has not been demonstrated; the confirmed finding is loss of the required anticipation behavior.

### R4 — medium — Published coordinator cost excludes fallback occupancy reports

Locations: `Assets/RoadRage/Features/Vehicles/Traffic/Lifecycle/TrafficV2StepRunner.cs:198` and `:207`. Sources: blind-hunter + edge-case-hunter.

`TrafficV2StepCost.CoordinatorMilliseconds` explicitly includes reports for actors without a driving step (`:21`). Those reports are built before `Coordinate` starts its timer and allocation baseline, so their cost is absent from both coordinator metrics. Total host-step cost still includes it; this does not prove a D13 overrun. Move the coordination measurement scope to include report collection or account for that section explicitly.
